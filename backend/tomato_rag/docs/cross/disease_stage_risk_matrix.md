## Most important control per stage
- **Nursery:** clean certified seed, insect-proof net, hand and tool hygiene.
- **Transplanting:** reject symptomatic seedlings; whitefly barrier and reflective mulch.
- **Vegetative:** airflow, drip, mulching, roguing of virus plants, weather-based protectant sprays.
- **Flowering / fruit set:** protectant program for Septoria and bacterial spot; avoid stress.
- **Fruit development:** protect the canopy and fruit; rotate fungicides; check PHI.
- **Ripening / harvest:** PHI, dry harvest, sanitation.
- **End of season:** remove crop with roots, disinfect, rotate.

## Chemical rotation principle
Do not repeat the same FRAC (fungicide) or IRAC (insecticide/miticide) group in consecutive sprays. Mix protectants (mancozeb, chlorothalonil, copper) with systemic partners. Always follow the label and CIB&RC registration for tomato, and observe pre-harvest intervals.
