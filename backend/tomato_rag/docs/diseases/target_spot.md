## Symptoms
- Small water-soaked or brown spots that enlarge into brown lesions with **concentric rings and a light-brown/tan centre**, often with a yellow halo; lesions are larger and more uniform than Septoria spots.
- Can occur on leaves at any height, including young leaves, unlike early blight which prefers older lower leaves.
- Lesions on stems and petioles; sunken, brown pitted spots on ripening fruit.
- Severe infection causes leaf yellowing and defoliation, more so in dense humid canopies.

## Favorable conditions
Warm, humid conditions (20-30 C) with prolonged leaf wetness and dense foliage; common in polyhouses and in humid, rainy periods. Fungus survives on debris and has a wide host range.

## Prevention
- Use healthy seedlings; rotate with non-host crops (avoid cucumber, papaya, brinjal, cotton, soybean in the immediate rotation as far as possible since this fungus has a wide host range).
- Space, stake and prune for airflow; avoid excess nitrogen that produces dense canopy.
- Drip irrigation; avoid wetting leaves.
- Clean tools; remove and destroy crop residues after harvest.

## Cultural control when disease is seen
- Remove infected leaves; thin canopy to dry it faster.
- Improve ventilation in polyhouses.

## Organic / biological control
- Copper-based sprays and Bacillus/Trichoderma products give limited protection when applied early.

## Chemical control (verify on CIB&RC; follow label dose, interval and PHI)
- Protectants: mancozeb (FRAC M03), chlorothalonil (M05).
- Systemic options where registered: azoxystrobin (FRAC 11), difenoconazole (FRAC 3), or their mixtures.
- Begin when first lesions appear and continue at label interval; alternate modes of action.

## Stage-wise action
| Stage | Risk | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Nursery | Low | Clean seedlings and trays |
| Transplanting | Low | Inspect plants; choose non-host field history |
| Vegetative | Medium | Maintain airflow; avoid excess nitrogen |
| Flowering/fruit set | Medium-High | Scout weekly; remove infected leaves |
| Fruit development | High | Protectant/systemic rotation; keep canopy open |
| Ripening/harvest | Medium-High | Observe PHI; discard spotted fruit |
| End of season | - | Destroy residues; rotate |

## Look-alikes
Early blight (older leaves first, distinct target rings), Septoria (tiny spots with dark border). See `cross/leaf_spot_comparison.md`.
