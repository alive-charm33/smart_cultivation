## What "healthy" means here
The classifier found no visible symptoms on the leaf image. A healthy leaf image does **not** guarantee that the whole plant is disease-free: early infection, root diseases, wilt, nutrient problems and internal virus infections may not show on one leaf.

## Weekly scouting checklist
- Look at the upper and lower surface of leaves (mites, whitefly, mold).
- Check lower leaves first (early blight, Septoria).
- Check young growing tips (virus symptoms, curling, mottling).
- Check stems and fruit for lesions.
- Look for whiteflies by tapping plants; check yellow sticky traps.
- Keep notes and photos for comparison with the previous week.

## General prevention for all stages
- Healthy certified seed and seedlings; net-covered nursery.
- Crop rotation (2-3 years away from tomato, potato, brinjal, chilli).
- Balanced nutrition with soil testing; adequate calcium and potassium; avoid excess nitrogen.
- Uniform irrigation (drip); avoid water stress and wet foliage.
- Stake, prune and space plants for airflow.
- Mulching to reduce soil splash and weeds.
- Sanitize tools and hands; remove weeds and debris.
- Use pesticides only when needed and rotate modes of action.

## Non-disease problems that may be confused with disease
- Blossom-end rot (calcium uptake problem from uneven watering).
- Nutrient deficiencies (yellowing of old leaves = nitrogen or magnesium; purple = phosphorus).
- Sunscald, heat stress, herbicide drift, fruit cracking.

## Stage-wise action
| Stage | Action |
|---|---|
| Nursery | Keep trays clean, net-covered, and well ventilated |
| Transplanting | Plant healthy seedlings; irrigate gently; start scouting |
| Vegetative | Weekly scouting; pruning, staking, balanced fertilizer |
| Flowering/fruit set | Prevent stress; monitor for pests and leaf spots |
| Fruit development | Consistent watering; potassium and calcium; watch for spots |
| Ripening/harvest | Harvest dry; clean crates; watch pre-harvest intervals |
| End of season | Remove residues; rotate field |

## When to ask for a new diagnosis
If new symptoms appear, upload a fresh, clear image of a single leaf in natural light, with both the upper and lower surface if possible.
