## Symptoms
- Numerous **small (about 2-3 mm) circular spots** with dark brown margins and **tan to gray centres**; tiny black dots (pycnidia) may be visible in the centres with a hand lens.
- Starts on **lower, older leaves** after first fruit set and moves upward.
- Heavily spotted leaves yellow, wither and fall; defoliation exposes fruit to sunscald.
- Stems and calyces may show spots; fruit is rarely affected.

## Favorable conditions
Warm (20-25 C), humid, rainy or overcast weather with frequent leaf wetness. Spreads by splashing water and by tools and hands. Survives on crop debris, solanaceous weeds (nightshade, horse nettle) and possibly seed.

## Prevention
- Use clean seedlings; rotate 2-3 years away from solanaceous crops.
- Remove solanaceous weeds and volunteer tomatoes.
- Stake, prune and space plants for airflow; mulch to stop soil splash.
- Drip irrigation; keep foliage dry.
- Sanitize tools; avoid working in wet foliage.
- Clean up and destroy debris after harvest; deep-plough or bury residues.

## Cultural control when disease is seen
- Remove infected lower leaves early and destroy them.
- Avoid overhead watering.

## Organic / biological control
- Copper-based products as protectants; Bacillus-based products offer partial suppression.

## Chemical control (verify on CIB&RC; follow label dose, interval and PHI)
- Protectants: chlorothalonil (FRAC M05), mancozeb (M03), copper oxychloride (M01).
- Systemic options where registered: azoxystrobin (FRAC 11), difenoconazole (FRAC 3).
- Begin at the first spots, particularly before or at first fruit set, and repeat at label interval during wet weather; rotate groups.

## Stage-wise action
| Stage | Risk | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Nursery | Low | Clean trays, avoid overhead watering |
| Transplanting | Low-Medium | Check seedlings; no spotted leaves |
| Vegetative | Medium | Remove weeds, mulch, prune lower leaves |
| Flowering/fruit set | High | Start protectant program at first spots |
| Fruit development | High | Maintain protection; keep canopy dry |
| Ripening/harvest | Medium | Observe PHI; avoid handling wet plants |
| End of season | - | Remove debris; rotate crop |

## Look-alikes
Early blight (big rings), bacterial spot (angular water-soaked, no gray centre). Septoria spots are small, round and numerous, with a gray centre. See `cross/leaf_spot_comparison.md`.
