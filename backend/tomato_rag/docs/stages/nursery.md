## Timing
Typically 21-30 days after sowing (DAS), until seedlings have 4-6 true leaves and are hardened for transplanting. Timing varies with variety, hybrid, season and temperature. Indeterminate hybrids flower, set fruit and ripen fruit at the same time, so more than one stage can apply. If the day count and what you see on the plant disagree, trust the plant.

## What happens
Seed germination, emergence and growth to 4-6 true leaves. Seedlings are small, tender and densely packed, so disease spreads quickly, and infections carried into the field affect the whole crop.

## Water and nutrient needs
Light, frequent watering of the media; avoid waterlogging. Use a sterile, well-drained medium (cocopeat or similar). Starter nutrition through the media or a mild soluble fertilizer after true leaves appear.

## Key management
- Use certified seed; apply recommended seed treatment.
- Raise seedlings in trays under **40-50 mesh insect-proof net** to exclude whiteflies.
- Sterilize trays and tools; do not reuse infested media.
- Ventilate to avoid damping-off and fungal problems; water in the morning.
- Harden seedlings 5-7 days before transplanting.

## Disease and pest risks at this stage
| Disease | Risk | Note |
|---|---|---|
| tylcv | Very high | Whitefly infection in nursery = severe loss; use nets and traps |
| tomato_mosaic_virus | High | Seed-borne and hand-spread; wash hands |
| bacterial_spot | High | Seed-borne; avoid overhead watering |
| early_blight | Medium | Collar rot; use clean seed and trays |
| late_blight | Low-Medium | Only in cool, wet weather; ventilate |
| leaf_mold | Low | Humid, unventilated nurseries |

## Decision rule
Do not transplant any seedling showing spots, curling, mottling or mold. See the matching `diseases/` document.
