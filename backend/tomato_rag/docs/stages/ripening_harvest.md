## Timing
Typically from about 65 days after transplanting (DAT); harvest continues for several weeks to months, until about day 110 or later in indeterminate hybrids. Timing varies with variety, hybrid, season and temperature. Indeterminate hybrids flower, set fruit and ripen fruit at the same time, so more than one stage can apply. If the day count and what you see on the plant disagree, trust the plant.

## What happens
Fruit changes color and is picked repeatedly. Picking continues while new fruit and leaves are still exposed to disease.

## Water and nutrient needs
Reduce but do not stop watering; avoid sudden heavy irrigation that causes cracking. Potassium remains important; avoid late nitrogen.

## Key management
- **Check PHI** before every spray; use only products with short PHI while harvesting.
- Harvest when foliage is dry; do not pick from wet plants (spreads bacteria and viruses).
- Discard diseased and lesioned fruit away from the field.
- Clean hands, crates and tools; wash crates between uses.
- Remove infected plants promptly.

## Disease and pest risks at this stage
| Disease | Risk | Note |
|---|---|---|
| late_blight | High | Fruit rot in cool, wet weather |
| early_blight | High | Stem-end fruit rot and defoliation |
| target_spot | Medium-High | Fruit pitting |
| bacterial_spot | Medium | Spread by handling wet plants |
| spider_mites | Medium-High | Late-season outbreaks |
| leaf_mold | Medium | Humidity in dense canopy |
